Privacy Policy — SmokeFree: Quit Smoking

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Data We Collect

SmokeFree stores your quit-smoking data (quit date, cigarettes per day, journal entries, profile name and photo) only on your device using Android DataStore. We do not upload your personal data to our servers. Your profile photo never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.

Advertising

The free version of SmokeFree shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Before ads load, you will see a Google consent form (UMP) where you can choose personalized or non-personalized ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID to serve ads. You can remove all ads with a one-time purchase. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

PRO Lifetime ($14.99) and Remove Ads ($4.99) are one-time purchases processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your payment information. Purchases are linked to your Google Play account and can be restored on any device.

Health Disclaimer

SmokeFree is a motivational and educational tracker, not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The breathing exercises and tips are for general wellness only. Consult a healthcare professional for personalized help quitting smoking.

Children's Privacy

SmokeFree is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
